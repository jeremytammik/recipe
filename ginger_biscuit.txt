mix

1½ cups of all-purpose flour
1 teaspoon baking soda
2 teaspoons ground ginger
pinch of salt

mix in 

½ cup of softened butter
½ cup of brown sugar
5 tablespoons of golden syrup

shape into balls
roll in sugar
bake at 180°c for about 12 minutes until golden.

instructions:

- preheat the oven to 190°c (170°c fan) or gas mark 5.
- grease a baking tray.
- in a saucepan, melt the butter, caster sugar, and golden syrup over medium heat. once melted, remove from heat and let it cool slightly.
- in a bowl, mix the self-raising flour, ground ginger, and bicarbonate of soda.
- combine the dry ingredients with the melted mixture to form a dough.
- roll the dough into small balls (about the size of a 50p coin) and place them on the greased baking tray, leaving space for spreading.
- flatten each ball slightly with a fork.
- bake for about 10 minutes or until golden brown.
- allow to cool on the tray for a few minutes before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely.
