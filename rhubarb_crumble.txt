## Rhubarb & Ginger Crisp For Two

Four stalks provide just enough volume for a cozy dessert for two people.

The Filling: Slice the rhubarb and toss with sugar and a teaspoon of freshly grated ginger (rhubarb’s best friend).

The Topping: Rub together equal parts flour, oats, brown sugar, and cold butter until crumbly.

The Bake: Pile it into a small baking dish and bake at 375°F (190°C) until the fruit is bubbling and the top is golden-brown.
